# Carrier Exceptions & SLA Guidelines (FedEx / UPS)

## 1. SLA Cutoffs
To meet expected delivery dates, packages must be loaded and scanned by the carrier before the following cutoff times:

| Carrier | Service Level | Cutoff Time (Local) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| FedEx | Express / Overnight | 18:30 (6:30 PM) |
| FedEx | Ground / Home Delivery | 16:00 (4:00 PM) |
| UPS | Next Day Air | 19:00 (7:00 PM) |
| UPS | Ground | 15:30 (3:30 PM) |

**Exception:** Peak season (Nov 1 - Dec 31) cutoffs are extended by 1 hour.

## 2. Lost-in-Transit Criteria
A package is considered "Lost in Transit" if it meets the following conditions:
*   **No Scan for 48 Hours:** The tracking history shows no movement or physical scans for 48 consecutive hours after the initial "Origin Scan".
*   **Weather/Force Majeure:** Exclude days where carrier service alerts indicate systemic delays due to weather or natural disasters.

## 3. Claims Filing Procedures
1.  Verify the package meets the "Lost-in-Transit" criteria or the customer has provided photographic evidence of damage.
2.  File claim via the respective carrier portal within 15 days of the original ship date.
3.  Required Documentation:
    *   Commercial Invoice
    *   Proof of replacement cost
    *   Damage photos (if applicable)

## 4. HAZMAT (Hazardous Materials) Limitations
*   **Lithium Batteries:** Must be shipped via Ground services ONLY if standalone. Next Day Air permitted only if batteries are installed in equipment (UN3481) and properly labeled.
*   **Aerosols / Flammables:** Restricted to Ground shipping. Maximum weight per carton: 30 lbs.
*   **Carrier Rejection:** If a carrier rejects a HAZMAT package due to improper labeling, the package must be routed to the Compliance Team for relabeling and re-induction. Do not attempt to reship without QA sign-off.
